## What I Built

TrailSense AI is an early-stage project exploring how local AI can encourage people to spend less time on screens and more time observing nature. The initial goal is to identify birds from photographs and keep a personal observation journal.

## How I Built It

The planned architecture combines a Java Spring Boot API, a Python inference service, and a locally loaded open-weight image model. The model will be selected after checking its license, species coverage, and local hardware requirements. This is a work in progress; these components are not all implemented yet.

## Why Does Open Innovation Matter?

Local inference can keep photographs on a user's device or local network, reduce dependence on paid cloud APIs, and make model experimentation possible. These benefits depend on the chosen model license and a successful network-disconnected test. I will document measured results and limitations rather than claim offline support before validating it.
